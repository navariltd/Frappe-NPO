# 6. Chapters, public pages and certifications

This chapter covers the three things that make a small organisation look organised to
the outside world: local chapters, a public website presence, and a directory of people
you have certified.

## 6.1 Chapters

A **chapter** is a local group of members, run by a chapter head, working in a region.

- Create a chapter, name it, and appoint a **chapter head** who is already a member.
- Add the chapter's members from your existing member list — nobody needs a second
  profile.
- Because chapters belong to regions, you can report membership and activity by area.
- A chapter head can be given website/portal access so they see their own chapter's
  people without seeing the rest of the organisation.

Use chapters when you have branches of *supporters*; use **Organisation** and **Partner**
(chapter 12) for agencies you deliver with. They are different relationships and keeping
them apart saves confusion later.

## 6.2 Certification

The certification feature is for the people and firms you accredit — trainers,
consultants, partner staff, community health workers.

1. Someone applies: you record the application with their name, email and currency, and
   track it as *Yet to appear*, *Certified* or *Not Certified*.
2. When they pass, you create a **Certified Consultant** record linked back to the
   application, with country, contact and the certificate details.
3. The consultant then appears on the public certification directory.

Because the applicant's email is a user account, the same person can log in later to
renew or update their own details.

## 6.3 Public pages

The app publishes several pages on your website; they need no developer once the content
is filled in:

| Page | What visitors can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Chapter pages** | See the chapter, its region, its head and its members, and get in touch. |
| **Certification directory** | Look up a certified consultant by name or country. |
| **Grant calls** | Read the current call, its terms and how to apply. |
| **Membership and donation forms** | Join, renew or give, and pay online. |

If a page shows nothing, the record behind it is usually not published — open the record
and check the website/publish setting, and that the route is enabled in the Website
settings.

## 6.4 Keeping the public side honest

- Only publish people who have agreed to be listed. The directory is public data.
- Unpublish a consultant when certification lapses; do not delete the record, or you
  lose the history of what they were certified for.
- Public membership and donation forms create real records and real payments, so test
  them with a small real payment before a campaign, not during one.
