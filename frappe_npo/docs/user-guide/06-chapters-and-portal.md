# 6. Chapters and certifications

This chapter covers the three things that make a small organisation look organised to
the outside world: local chapters and a directory of people
you have certified.

## 6.1 Chapters

A **chapter** is a local group of members, run by a chapter head, working in a region.

- Create a chapter, name it, and appoint a **chapter head** who is already a member.
- Add the chapter's members from your existing member list — nobody needs a second
  profile.
- Because chapters belong to regions, you can report membership and activity by area.
- A chapter head can be given website/portal access so they see their own chapter's
  people without seeing the rest of the organisation.

Use chapters when you have branches of _supporters_; use **Organisation** and **Partner**
(chapter 12) for agencies you deliver with. They are different relationships and keeping
them apart saves confusion later.

## 6.2 Certification

The certification feature is for the people and firms you accredit — trainers,
consultants, partner staff, community health workers.

1. Someone applies: you record the application with their name, email and currency, and
   track it as _Yet to appear_, _Certified_ or _Not Certified_.
2. When they pass, you create a **Certified Consultant** record linked back to the
   application, with country, contact and the certificate details.
3. The consultant then appears on the public certification directory.

Because the applicant's email is a user account, the same person can log in later to
renew or update their own details.
