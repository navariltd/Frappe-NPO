# 5. Volunteers

Volunteers are the third kind of supporter, after members and donors: people who give
time. The app keeps a register you can actually plan a week with.

## 5.1 What you can do

- Register each volunteer with contact details, photo and ID, organised by branch, so
  you know who is local to a site.
- Group them by **Volunteer Type** — teacher, driver, cook, counsellor, medical
  volunteer. Each type can carry the standard amount attached to that kind of
  volunteering (a daily stipend, for example), so payouts are consistent whoever
  processes them.
- Record **availability**: whether they help weekly, on weekdays or at weekends, and
  which part of the day they are free.
- List **skills** in free text, in the words your team actually uses — "can teach Class
  5 maths", "knows the local dialect", "first aid trained".
- Keep a note of anything the co-ordinator must know: languages, physical constraints,
  emergency contact.
- Hold a full address and contact record for letters, ID cards and emergencies.

## 5.2 Using the register

- **Before a camp or a drive**, filter by availability and branch, then call the people
  who are free. That is the entire point of recording availability — if nobody fills it
  in, the register is a phone book.
- **When a volunteer stops**, keep the record and note it. Volunteer history is evidence
  for funders, and useful when someone comes back after a year.
- **Stipends and reimbursements** to volunteers are paid through the normal payment
  route, so they appear in the same books as every other cost and can be reported per
  project.
- **Volunteer hours and attendance** are not tracked automatically; if a funder needs
  them, log them as daily visit updates or keep a register sheet — do not duplicate the
  volunteer record.

## 5.3 Where volunteers meet the rest of the app

| You are doing | Volunteers help because |
| --- | --- |
| Recruitment phases and intake drives | you can staff the camp from the availability list |
| Awareness and health camps | camp records capture attendance and outcomes |
| Food distribution | the food team role and volunteer skills line up with the roster |
| Shelter and care centres | volunteers are often the daily-visit and activity staff |
